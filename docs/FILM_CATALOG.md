# Film catalog

`src/data/film_catalog.json` is generated by `scripts/build_catalog.py`.

* Box speeds are the values in the product specification. P3200 and Delta 3200 store their designation, not an ISO box speed.
* Formats and reciprocity data were transcribed from manufacturer publications (Ilford/Harman power law; Kodak F-4016/F-4017/F-4043 tables; Kodak "no correction to 1 s" for Portra 160/400 and Ektar; Fujifilm Provia 100F) but **have not yet been re-checked against current datasheets**. Every record therefore has `lastVerified: null` and a `verificationNotes` entry. Check them and fill in `lastVerified` before relying on them.
* Stocks without known data are `unpublished`: no correction is invented and the app says so.
* Kentmere Pan 800 is marked `unverified` (included at the specified speed; product details unconfirmed).
* Rollei, Adox, ORWO and Harman are declared but empty, waiting for verified records.

**Updates:** bump `catalogVersion`, edit the generator, publish the JSON anywhere over HTTPS, then Settings → Film catalog updates → URL or file. Merging validates the file, shows new / announced / discontinued items, and never removes stocks.
